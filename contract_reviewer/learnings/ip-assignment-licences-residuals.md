# §3 IP: assignment, licences, background IP, feedback, residuals

Compiled 2026-10-10 (as-of dates inline). Section numbers §1-§8 refer to the topic files listed in INDEX.md.

_Attribution: paraphrases and cites the Common Paper standard agreements (commonpaper.com, CC BY 4.0) and the Bonterms standard agreements (bonterms.com, CC BY 4.0); pinned snapshots are listed in gaps-and-sources.md. Changes: summarized, compared and reorganized by Manigent Contributors._

**Consult when:** a services, development or SaaS contract allocates ownership of work product or grants rights in information or feedback.

**Bonterms PSA v1.2 model** ([§§2.5, 5–6, 11](https://bonterms.com/standard/professional-services-agreement-v1.2)).
- Ownership is a cover-page choice, and an SOW can override it per deliverable.
- **Assigned Deliverables:** irrevocable present assignment, work-for-hire where lawful, moral-rights waiver, further assurances, power of attorney.
- **Licensed Deliverables:** a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable licence.
- **Reserved Materials** (provider pre-existing content listed in the SOW) are not assigned. Where they are embedded in deliverables, they are licensed instead.
- Third-party materials cleared at provider's cost; open source listed; copyleft needs SOW approval.
- Licences are treated as "intellectual property" licences under 11 U.S.C. §365(n).
- There is no licence-back of customer materials.

**Rule-level checks.**
- Limit any assignment to IP *created in performing the services under this agreement*. Language that reaches beyond the engagement captures background IP. Fix it with a pre-existing / independently developed carve-out plus a licence to any background IP embedded in deliverables.
- Use "hereby assigns" (a bare promise to assign may need later conveyance), and keep a backup assignment because work-for-hire covers non-employees only in statutory categories.
- **Licence-back.** A provider that cedes ownership usually needs rights to its generic know-how and reusable components. Bonterms achieves this through Reserved Materials.

**Feedback.** Both SaaS standards let the provider use feedback freely ([CP §1.4](https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/2.1), unchanged in 3.0; [Bonterms §15.2](https://bonterms.com/standard/bonterms-cloud-terms-v1)). Bonterms adds that the provider may not attribute feedback to the customer publicly. Resist any feedback clause that assigns ownership or covers confidential information.

**Residuals.** A residuals clause permits use of information retained in people's unaided memory. **Neither standard NDA, nor the Bonterms PSA, contains one** (see the §1 links).
- Disclosers resist residuals because memory cannot be audited, so the clause works as a practical licence to use CI and can erode trade-secret status.
- **Position when we are the discloser, especially to a competitor or a company that builds the same kind of product: strike it; walk-away.** Say so as the position. Do not present "delete or narrow" as equal options; narrowing is not offered to a competitor.
- **Fallbacks only when we are the net recipient, or the discloser is not a competitor and the business insists:** exclude trade secrets, source code, personal data, financials and roadmaps; no intentional memorizing; no patent or copyright licence; no use in competing products.
