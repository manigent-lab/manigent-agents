# §7 Assignment, change of control, convenience termination, law and venue

Compiled 2026-10-10 (as-of dates inline). Section numbers §1-§8 refer to the topic files listed in INDEX.md.

_Attribution: paraphrases and cites the Common Paper standard agreements (commonpaper.com, CC BY 4.0) and the Bonterms standard agreements (bonterms.com, CC BY 4.0); pinned snapshots are listed in gaps-and-sources.md. Changes: summarized, compared and reorganized by Manigent Contributors._

**Consult when:** you are reviewing transferability, exit rights, or forum boilerplate.

- **Assignment.** Both standards require consent, except that either party may assign in a merger, reorganisation, or sale of substantially all assets or voting securities (CP: on notice). A non-permitted assignment is void ([CP §12.6](https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/2.1), same number and text in 3.0; [Bonterms §22.1](https://bonterms.com/standard/bonterms-cloud-terms-v1)).
  - Check that the M&A carve-out runs to **both** parties.
  - Customer fallback: a termination right if the agreement is assigned to a direct competitor.
  - Change-of-control triggers may sit outside the assignment clause, so search for "control".
- **Termination for convenience.** Neither SaaS standard gives a mid-term right (Bonterms: only when no subscription is in effect).
  - **Services:** the Bonterms PSA lets the *Customer* end an SOW on **30 days'** notice after paying amounts incurred to date. The Provider has no such right ([PSA §11](https://bonterms.com/standard/professional-services-agreement-v1.2)).
- **Law and venue.** Cover-page variables with exclusive jurisdiction; Bonterms has no default, so a blank is an open issue. CP §12.4: injunctions for confidentiality/IP breaches in any court, no bond. Minnesota and California limit foreign law/forum for in-state workers (§4).
