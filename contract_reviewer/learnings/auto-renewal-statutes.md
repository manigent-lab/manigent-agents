# §6 Auto-renewal statutes

Compiled 2026-10-10 (as-of dates inline). Section numbers §1-§8 refer to the topic files listed in INDEX.md.

**Consult when:** a contract auto-renews (B2B services or equipment, or consumer subscriptions). Table with numbers and sources: `knowledge/auto-renewal-statutes.tsv`.

**NY GOL [§5-903](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/5-903)** applies to contracts for **service, maintenance or repair** of real or personal property. It is not consumer-limited, so B2B contracts are covered under NY law.
- The renewal clause is **unenforceable** against the recipient unless the provider gives written notice, **personally or by certified mail**, **15–30 days** before the recipient's non-renewal deadline, calling attention to the clause.
- It does not apply to renewal periods of one month or less.
- Email alone is not a listed method.
- Whether a SaaS subscription counts as a "service" contract is fact-specific, so escalate.

**NY GOL [§5-901](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/5-901)** applies to **leases of personal property** with auto-renewal. Same rule: written notice served personally or **by mail**, 15–30 days before the lessee's deadline. Renewal periods of one month or less are excepted.

**Wisconsin [§134.49](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/134/49)** is B2B: business-equipment leases and business services for a customer doing business in Wisconsin (many exclusions, e.g. usage contracts with a $250,000+ 12-month minimum). An auto-renewal over one month needs a signed or initialled conspicuous disclosure; if both initial and renewal terms exceed a year, written notice **15-60 days** before the decline deadline. Otherwise the renewal clause is unenforceable and the contract ends at term end; damages and fees available.

**Minnesota [§325G.57](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/325G.57)** (consumers): notice of a material change before it takes effect, with cancellation instructions, or the change is void; free trials over 30 days need a reminder 5-30 days before they end; annual reminder by mail or email; online cancellation (§325G.60).

**California ARL, B&P §17600 et seq.**
- **Scope:** **consumers**, meaning individuals buying for personal or household use ([§17601](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/business-and-professions-code/bpc-sect-17601/)). Pure B2B contracts are generally outside it.
- **AB 2863** applies to contracts entered into, amended or extended on or after **July 1, 2025** ([§17602](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/business-and-professions-code/bpc-sect-17602/); [DWT](https://www.dwt.com/insights/2024/10/ab-2863-updates-california-automatic-renewal-law)). Requirements:
  - **express affirmative consent** to the renewal terms;
  - proof of consent kept for **3 years, or 1 year after termination if longer**;
  - free-to-paid trials are covered.
- **Pre-renewal notice:**
  - initial term of 1 year or more: **15–45 days** before renewal;
  - trial or promotional price lasting more than 31 days: **3–21 days** before it ends.
- **Also:** annual reminder for annual plans (§17602(h)); **price-change notice no less than 7 and no more than 30 days** before the change takes effect, stating how to cancel (§17602(g)(2)); online cancellation for online sign-ups; save offers only alongside a "click to cancel" option.

**B2B vs B2C.** For enterprise SaaS, the live risk is usually NY §5-903. For self-serve plans sold to individuals, assume the ARL applies. The FTC's 2024 "click-to-cancel" amendments were vacated by the 8th Circuit in July 2025; the same Feb 12, 2026 notice that removed the non-compete rule restored 16 CFR part 425 to its pre-2024 text ([91 FR 6507](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-12/html/2026-02866.htm)). The FTC reopened negative-option rulemaking with an advance notice on March 11, 2026 (secondary source); there is no federal click-to-cancel rule in force.
