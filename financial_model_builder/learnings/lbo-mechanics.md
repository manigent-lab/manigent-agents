## LBO mechanics
**Consult when:** building a leveraged buyout model.
- **Sources and uses:**
  - Uses = purchase equity value + refinanced debt + transaction fees + financing fees + minimum cash.
  - Sources = new debt tranches + sponsor equity + rollover + existing cash used.
  - The two must balance. Sponsor equity is the plug.
- **Fees:**
  - M&A/advisory fees are expensed at close (they hit equity, not goodwill).
  - Financing fees (OID, arrangement fees) are capitalized against debt and amortized over the loan life as non-cash interest. This affects book interest, not cash.
- **Debt schedule:**
  - Order tranches by seniority: revolver, term loan A/B, senior notes, sub/PIK.
  - Cash sweep = a percentage of excess cash flow after mandatory payments and minimum cash, applied to prepayable tranches in order.
  - Mandatory amortization first; revolver covers shortfalls; PIK accretes to principal.
  - Floating rate = base rate (SOFR) + margin, often with a floor.
- **Returns:**
  - MOIC = total equity proceeds / total equity invested.
  - Use IRR for evenly spaced periods. Use XIRR (actual/365) for dated flows, dividends and recaps.
  - Exit equity = exit EV (multiple × exit-year EBITDA) − net debt at exit − exit fees.
  - **Interest-limit check:** apply §163(j) at 30% of ATI with D&A added back (post-2024) when computing cash taxes.
