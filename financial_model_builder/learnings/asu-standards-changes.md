## Standards changes that move models (2023–2026 ASUs)
**Consult when:** a forecast depends on line-item granularity, tax footnotes, segment data or capitalization policy.
- **ASU 2023-07 segments:** annual periods for fiscal years beginning after 2023-12-15, and interims for fiscal years beginning after 2024-12-15. Applied retrospectively. It requires the significant segment expenses regularly provided to the CODM and the CODM's title, and it applies to single-segment filers too. — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2024/sec-reporting-accounting.pdf), as of 2024.
- **ASU 2023-08 crypto:** fair value through net income for in-scope crypto assets, for fiscal years beginning after 2024-12-15. — [PwC Viewpoint](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/effective_dates_of_f/public_companies/public_companies_US/standards_effective__US.html).
- **ASU 2023-09 income taxes:** annual periods beginning after 2024-12-15 for PBEs. Rate reconciliation in specified categories, both amount and percent, with a 5% threshold. Income taxes paid split into federal, state and foreign, plus any jurisdiction at or above 5% of the total. Use the cash-taxes-paid disclosure to calibrate the cash tax rate separately from the book ETR — [Cherry Bekaert summary](https://www.cbh.com/insights/articles/asu-2023-09-fasbs-income-tax-rule-for-public-companies/), as of 2025.
- **ASU 2024-03 / 2025-01 DISE:** annual reporting for fiscal years beginning after 2026-12-15, interims after 2027-12-15. Relevant expense captions are disaggregated into purchases of inventory, employee compensation, D&A and intangible amortization. Selling expenses are also disclosed. Until adoption, opex-by-nature splits stay an estimate.
- **ASU 2024-04:** induced conversions of convertibles, fiscal years beginning after 2025-12-15.
- **2025 ASUs** (PBE dates, from [PwC Viewpoint](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/effective_dates_of_f/public_companies/public_companies_US/standards_effective__US.html)):
  2025-02 SAB 122 (on issuance); 2025-05 credit losses on receivables (FY beginning after 2025-12-15); 2025-03 VIE acquirer, 2025-04 share-based customer consideration, 2025-07 derivatives scope, 2025-08 purchased loans, 2025-09 hedging, 2025-12 codification (all after 2026-12-15); 2025-06 internal-use software and 2025-11 interim reporting (after 2027-12-15); 2025-10 government grants (after 2028-12-15).
- **2026 ASUs:** ASU 2026-01 (initial measurement of PIK dividends on equity-classified preferred, Topic 505) was issued 2026-04-23 and is effective for annual periods beginning after 2026-12-15 — [Deloitte Heads Up](https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/publications/deloitte/heads-up/2026/fasb-pik-dividends-equity-classified-preferred-stock). ASU 2026-02 (environmental credits) is reported as issued 2026-05; see Gaps.
